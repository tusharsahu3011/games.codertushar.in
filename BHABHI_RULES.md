# 🃏 Bhabhi (Thulla / Get Away) — Complete Game Rules & Guide

> **"Bhabhi"** (also popularly known as **Thulla**, **Thoko**, or **Get Away**) is one of the most iconic, competitive, and entertaining traditional Indian card evasion games. 
> 
> Unlike typical trick-taking card games where players fight to win points, in **Bhabhi**, **the sole goal is to get rid of all your cards and NOT be the last player standing**. The last player left holding cards loses the match and receives the humorous title of **"Bhabhi"**!

---

## 📌 1. Game Overview & Objectives

| Property | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Players** | 3 to 6 Players (4 to 5 players is ideal) |
| **Deck** | Standard 52-card deck (No Jokers) |
| **Card Hierarchy** | **A (High) > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 (Low)** |
| **Objective** | Shed all cards from your hand. Avoid being the last player left with cards. |
| **Loser** | The last player holding any cards becomes the **Bhabhi**! |

---

## 🎴 2. Card Ranks & Suits

The game uses standard international suits with traditional colors:
- **Spades (♠)** — Black (`#0f172a`)
- **Clubs (♣)** — Black (`#0f172a`)
- **Hearts (♥)** — Red (`#dc2626`)
- **Diamonds (♦)** — Red (`#dc2626`)

### Card Value Ranking (Highest to Lowest):
$$\text{Ace (A)} > \text{King (K)} > \text{Queen (Q)} > \text{Jack (J)} > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2$$

> [!NOTE]
> Aces are always strictly high in Bhabhi. There is no concept of Ace being low (1).

---

## 🎲 3. Setup & The Deal

1. The dealer shuffles the full 52-card deck thoroughly.
2. All cards are dealt clockwise, one card at a time, to all players until the entire deck is exhausted.
3. Depending on the player count, hands may differ by 1 card (this is completely normal):
   - **3 Players**: 17, 17, 18 cards
   - **4 Players**: 13 cards each (exact)
   - **5 Players**: Two players get 11 cards, three get 10 cards
   - **6 Players**: Four players get 9 cards, two get 8 cards
4. Players organize their hand secretly by suits and ranks.

---

## ⚔️ 4. Gameplay & Trick Mechanics

### Round 1: The Mandatory Opening Lead
- The game always commences with the player who was dealt the **Ace of Spades (♠ A)**.
- That player **must** play the **Ace of Spades (♠ A)** face up into the center to begin the match.
- Moving clockwise, every player who holds a Spade **must** play a Spade card.

---

### Regular Turns & The "Lead Suit" Rule
- The first card placed down in any trick determines the **Lead Suit** for that trick.
- **Mandatory Follow-Suit Rule**:
  - If you hold **any** card of the lead suit in your hand, you **MUST** play a card of that suit.
  - You are free to choose *any* rank of that lead suit (high or low).
  - You **cannot** play another suit if you have even one card of the lead suit.

---

### The "Thulla" (or "Thoko") — The Penalty Hit! 💥
This is the heart and drama of Bhabhi!

1. When it is your turn and you **DO NOT HAVE** any card of the active lead suit, you have the right to play **ANY card of a different suit**.
2. Playing an off-suit card onto the table is called **striking a "Thulla"** (or **"Thoko"**).
3. **The Penalty**:
   - The trick immediately stops.
   - The game inspects all cards played so far in that trick of the **original lead suit**.
   - The player who placed the **HIGHEST card of the lead suit** is penalised!
   - That unfortunate player must **pick up ALL cards played in that trick** and add them into their hand!
4. **Who Leads Next?**:
   - The player who struck the Thulla earns the right to lead the next trick with any card of their choice.

---

### Clean Trick (The Sweep) ✨
- If **every active player** follows suit and plays a card of the lead suit (nobody cuts with a Thulla):
  - The player who played the **highest card of the lead suit** wins the trick.
  - All cards from that trick are gathered and placed face-down into the **Discard Pile**.
  - **These swept cards are permanently out of the match.**
  - The winner of the trick gets the lead to start the next trick.

---

## 🏆 5. Getting Away (Safe Exits)

- As soon as a player plays their very last card and does **not** pick up a Thulla penalty:
  - That player has successfully **"Got Away"**!
  - They are declared **Safe** and exit the game as a winner.
  - The order of exits is tracked (1st Safe, 2nd Safe, 3rd Safe...).
- **Passing the Lead**:
  - If a player empties their hand while leading or winning a trick, they cannot lead the next trick because they have no cards left.
  - In this case, the turn to lead passes to the **next active player seated clockwise** who still holds cards.

---

## 👑 6. Declaring the "Bhabhi" (Game End)

- The game continues through multiple tricks, sweeps, and Thullas until players shed their hands one by one.
- When all players except **ONE** have cleared their cards:
  - The last remaining player holding cards is officially the **BHABHI**!
  - The match ends immediately.
  - Traditionally, the Bhabhi must:
    - Deal cards for the subsequent match.
    - Suffer humorous teasing and give chai/samosa party treats to the winners!

## 🧠 7. Pro Strategies & Tactics

### 1. The Suit-Void Strategy (Fast Thulla Setup)
If you are dealt only 1 or 2 cards of a particular suit (e.g., only one Diamond: ♦ 3), try to dump them early when someone else leads Diamonds. Once you are completely out of Diamonds, you can hit anyone who leads Diamonds later with a heavy Thulla!

### 2. Guarding High Cards
Holding an Ace or King of a suit when opponents might be void in that suit is extremely dangerous. If you lead an Ace and an opponent doesn't have that suit, they will hit you with a Thulla and you will be forced to pick up the entire pile!

### 3. Dumping Heavy Cards on Safe Sweeps
When someone leads a suit and you have high cards (like K or Q) that you cannot win with, discard them safely if someone has already played an Ace above you! The Ace owner takes the lead, and you successfully dispose of your dangerous cards.

### 4. Reading the Table
Watch which players are unable to follow suit. Once you know Player B has no Clubs, never lead Clubs unless you want Player B to Thulla the player holding the highest Club!

## 📋 Summary of Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bhabhi** | The loser of the game — the last player remaining with cards. |
| **Thulla / Thoko** | Playing an off-suit card when you have none of the lead suit, penalizing the highest lead card holder. |
| **Get Away** | Emptying your hand completely and escaping Bhabhi status. |
| **Lead Suit** | The suit of the first card played in an active trick. |
| **Sweep** | A round where all players follow suit; all cards go to the discard pile. |
| **Discard Pile** | Dead cards permanently removed from the match after clean sweeps. |
