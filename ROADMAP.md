# 🗺️ Product Architecture & Roadmap — Fun With Dost Yaar

> **Application:** `Fun With Dost Yaar`  
> **Ecosystem:** Modern Social, Party & Card Game Platform  
> **Status:** Active & Playable  

---

## 1. Product Vision

**Fun With Dost Yaar** is engineered as a unified social and party game portal. Friends, families, and remote groups can play social deception games and traditional card games seamlessly across desktop and mobile devices.

Key Pillars:
1. **Multi-Game Extensibility:** A modular game registry that enables adding new titles (*Spyfall*, *Bhabhi*, *Decrypto*, *Codenames*, *Avalon*) without re-architecting navigation or rooms.
2. **Dual Deployment Modes:** Zero-friction **Pass & Play (Local Single Device)** and real-time **Online Multiplayer (Custom Rooms with WebRTC Voice)**.
3. **Future Accounts System (Coming Soon):** Custom player profiles, avatars, match history, and friend lists.
